{%- comment -%} "More episodes like this one" for an episode page. Every episode already links to its topic hubs, but the hubs are one hop away and the only episode-to-episode links are the two chronological neighbours. That leaves the archive shaped like a chain: an episode from 2019 is reachable only by walking it. These links cross-connect episodes that share a subject, which is how a reader (and a crawler) gets from any episode to the rest of the ones that answer the same kind of question. The window is centred on this episode's own position within its topic rather than always showing the newest episodes in that topic. Taking the newest would point every one of the 500+ pages at the same handful of episodes and leave the back catalogue with no inbound links at all.
