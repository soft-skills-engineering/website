In this episode, Dave and Jamison answer these questions:

1. Hiiii
   My question is about how to get faster at completing tasks. This is my first job as a software developer, and although I can do the work, everything seems to take me much longer than expected.
   
   For example, implementing 5 unit tests and 3 integration tests might look like a 30-minute task, but it ends up taking me an entire day!
   
   My manager has started bringing this up, asking why I'm so slow and what's taking so long. Honestly, it's making me stressed and worried about losing my job.
   
   Any advice on how to actually become more efficient?
   
   P.S. I use vim BTW. It doesn't help xD

2. Hey! I work at a huge international corporate. I joined two years ago on a team that sat in the same office as me, and everything was great. Then we went through a major reorg and I ended up on a different team. That happens, life goes on. The catch is that this team sits in an office in a very different timezone. There's a 7-hour difference, and I'm the only one not in that office. How do I handle this setup? Shifting my working hours isn't feasible for me, so in the best case I'd only get about 2 hours of overlap with my team. Any tips on how to integrate into the new team and keep some human interaction going beyond just work talk?
