So I finally flipped some metaphorical fingers and quit.
   Since then I’ve been interviewing almost exclusively with startups… except Google, where I somehow ended up with an offer on a team I’d genuinely enjoy.
   You’ve both bounced between big tech and startups—if you were in my shoes, how would you think about choosing between the two?
